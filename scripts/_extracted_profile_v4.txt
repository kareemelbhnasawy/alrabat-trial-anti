Alrabat Foundation delivers comprehensive foundation and ground support solutions, covering the 
full scope of works from site preparation to deep foundations and slope stabilization. The division 
supports residential, commercial, industrial, and infrastructure developments by providing safe, 
efficient, and technically reliable foundation systems tailored to project and ground conditions.
Enabling works form the essential ﬁrst stage of 
construction, ensuring sites are properly 
prepared for foundation and structural 
activities. Services include site preparation, 
fencing and hoarding, temporary facilities, 
vegetation removal, site leveling, excavation 
and earthworks, construction of temporary 
access roads, and diversion or protection of 
existing utilities.
The division provides a wide range of 
temporary and permanent shoring solutions to 
support excavations and prevent soil 
movement during construction. Shoring 
systems are designed to suit site constraints, 
excavation depth, and ground conditions, 
ensuring stability and safety throughout 
construction.

Piling forms a core part of the division’s 
expertise, delivering deep foundation solutions 
to achieve required load-bearing capacity and 
structural stability. A variety of piling 
techniques are applied to suit soil conditions, 
project requirements, and environmental 
considerations.
Services include driven piles, bored piles with 
large diameters and deep installation

rock formations.
Services include soil nailing, ground anchors, 
rock bolting, and tiebacks, as well as slope 
stabilization measures such as retaining walls, 
terracing, erosion control systems, vegetation 
solutions, and drainage systems to manage 
water pressure and prevent soil erosion.

Alrabat Marine & Coastal Works specializes in the design, construction, and maintenance of 
marine and coastal infrastructure, delivering reliable and technically strong solutions for ports, 
marinas, and coastal protection projects.
The division’s capabilities include quay wall and breakwater construction, marina development, 
port repair and maintenance, and dredging works to optimize navigation channels and harbor 
depths. Advanced dredging techniques and equipment are employed to achieve high precision 
while minimizing environmental impact.
Al Rabat also delivers beach improvement and coastal works focused on shoreline stabilization, 
erosion control, and enhanced public accessibility. In addition, specialized concrete repair services 
are provided to restore structural integrity, mitigate corrosion, and extend the service life of critical 
marine structures.
Supported by skilled technical teams, specialized marine equipment, and strict HSE and quality 
standards, the division delivers marine and coastal projects efficiently, safely, and sustainably.
